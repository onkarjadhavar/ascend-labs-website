Privacy Policy

Plain-English privacy standards. We treat your personal and student data with the care, discretion, and respect we would expect for our own.

EFFECTIVE: SEPTEMBER 2026 JURISDICTION: INDIA ZERO DATA BROKERAGE
Summary in Plain English: We only collect the information you choose to enter into our enquiry form (such as your name, email, phone number, and college or business name). We use it strictly to communicate with you about your application, web project, or notes notification. We never sell or share your information with advertisers.

1. Introduction & Operating Identity

This Privacy Policy describes how Ascend Labs ("we", "our", or "the collective") handles personal information collected through our website. Ascend Labs operates as an independent early-stage technology collective based in Maharashtra, India. The name "Ascend Labs" is a working brand identity; statutory company registration details will be incorporated upon formal establishment.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require account registration or collect passive tracking data to browse our website. The only personal data we collect is information you voluntarily provide through our contact forms:

  • Identity & Contact Details: Full name, email address, and optional phone or WhatsApp number.
  • Academic & Organizational Context: College or institute name and branch (for student internships, ambassador roles, and notes alerts) or business name (for website development enquiries).
  • Enquiry Details: Selected area of interest, custom notes, questions, or project briefs you submit.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To review, evaluate, and respond to your application for the 1-Month IT Internship Programme or College Ambassador Programme.
  • To provide design consultations, scope estimates, and project quotes for website development services.
  • To send email or WhatsApp updates regarding engineering notes drops for your specific university (DBATU/SPPU) and semester when requested.
  • To resolve technical queries or direct communications sent to our email addresses.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertising brokers under any circumstances.

5. Cookies & Analytics

Our website is engineered with lightweight static architecture. We do not use invasive tracking cookies, pixel trackers, or cross-site behavioral marketing tags.

6. Data Retention & Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the information we hold about you, or to request its complete deletion from our records at any time. Simply send an email to contact@ascendlabs.in with the subject line "Privacy Request".
