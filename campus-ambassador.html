02 / CAMPUS LEADERSHIP

Lead developer culture
on your campus.

Our College Ambassador programme empowers proactive student leaders to represent Ascend Labs, initiate peer learning circles, share technical opportunities, and bridge academia with modern industry practices.

COMMITMENT: 3-5 HOURS / WEEK TENURE: 1 SEMESTER (RENEWABLE) FOCUS: DBATU, SPPU & MAHARASHTRA COLLEGES

YOUR CORE RESPONSIBILITIES

How you will lead.

As an ambassador, you are the voice of your campus and the primary point of contact for technical initiatives and engineering notes distribution.

PILLAR 01

Community Catalyst

Establish informal peer study groups, organize virtual or campus-based tech discussions, and foster a culture of building projects instead of just memorizing theory.

OUTCOME: Campus tech visibility & peer collaboration
PILLAR 02

Knowledge & Notes Hub

Gather syllabus pain points from your classmates, beta-test upcoming DBATU/SPPU revision notes, and ensure high-quality academic outlines reach students who need them.

OUTCOME: Authentic feedback on student challenges
PILLAR 03

Opportunity Gateway

Share early cohort notifications for our 1-month IT internships, open technical workshops, and hackathons directly with your departmental student forums.

OUTCOME: Direct pathway for peers into real IT projects

AMBASSADOR PRIVILEGES

Leadership privileges & perks.

We invest directly in our campus ambassadors because you represent our collective on the ground.

  • ✦
    1-on-1 Founder Mentorship: Direct access to the founders for career advice, resume reviews, and engineering roadmap discussions.
  • ✦
    Fast-Track Internship Consideration: Priority admission into our intensive 1-month IT project internships.
  • ✦
    Official Letter of Recommendation (LOR): Verifiable leadership documentation highlighting your community and organizational contributions.
  • ✦
    Cross-College Peer Network: Collaborate with other active student leaders across DBATU, SPPU, and regional engineering universities.
  • ✦
    Leadership Credential: A formal Certificate of Campus Leadership you can proudly showcase on your LinkedIn and portfolio.
  • ✦
    Free Early Access: Exclusive access to all curated revision guides, roadmap templates, and internal dev workshops.

WHO SHOULD APPLY

Eligibility criteria.

  • ✓
    Enrolled Student: Currently pursuing an engineering degree (preferably 2nd, 3rd, or final year) at a recognized institution.
  • ✓
    Active Communicator: Comfortable speaking with classmates, professors, and student club coordinators.
  • ✓
    Reliable & Self-Driven: Able to manage 3–5 flexible hours weekly without impacting academic standing.
  • ✓
    Community Mindset: Genuine desire to help your fellow batchmates succeed and elevate campus engineering culture.
